V.I.T.A.L. Frameworks: Interdependent, Independent, and Hardship

Interdependent

Definition

Dependent upon one another; mutually dependent.

Origin: Merriam-Webster

Rule: The LOVE Framework

To sustain an interdependent relationship, apply these four principles:

  • L Loyalty – Commitment to the mutual bond and shared success.
  • O Observation – Attunement to the partner’s needs and changes in the environment.
  • V Vigilance – Protecting the relationship from external threats and complacency.
  • E Enlighten – Sharing knowledge, insights, and growth with one another.

Example

In a team project, each member relies on the others for success. If one person fails, the whole group suffers. By applying Loyalty to the team goal, Observing when a teammate is struggling, Vigilance against outside distractions, and Enlightening each other with new insights, the team maintains its interdependent strength.

Independent

Definition

Not dependent: such as; not subject to control by others : self-governing; not affiliated with a larger controlling unit.

Origin: Merriam-Webster

Rule: The GRIP Framework

Independence is the power to guide oneself into a state of self-determination:

  • G Guide – Direct your own path and decisions without external reliance.
  • R Rage – Channel the energy against constraints or lack of autonomy.
  • I Into – Push forward into new territories of self-reliance.
  • P Power – Establish your own authority and capacity to act.

Example

A startup founder who refuses venture capital to maintain independence. They Guide their own vision, channel their Rage against corporate constraints, push Into new markets, and establish their own Power structure.

Hardship

Definition

Privation, suffering; something that causes or entails suffering or privation.

Origin: Merriam-Webster

Rule: The LEAPS Technique

When facing or helping others through Hardship, use this framework for effective communication and support:

  • L Listen – Give full attention to the person suffering.
  • E Empathize – Validate their feelings and experience.
  • A Ask Questions – Clarify the root causes and needs.
  • P Paraphrase – Reflect back what you heard to ensure understanding.
  • S Summarize – Outline next steps or key takeaways to alleviate suffering.

Example

A counselor helping a client through financial Hardship. They Listen to the client’s story, Empathize with the stress, Ask Questions to clarify the root causes, Paraphrase to ensure understanding, and Summarize the next steps to alleviate suffering.
