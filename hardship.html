Hardship

Privation, Suffering & The LEAPS Technique

Topic

Hardship — enduring privation or suffering, and supporting others who are.

Definition

Privation, suffering; something that causes or entails suffering or privation.

Origin: Merriam-Webster →

Rule: The LEAPS Technique

When facing or helping others through hardship, LEAPS is the rule for effective communication and support.

  • LListen — give full attention to the person suffering.
  • EEmpathize — validate their feelings and experience.
  • AAsk Questions — clarify the root causes and real needs.
  • PParaphrase — reflect back what you heard to confirm understanding.
  • SSummarize — outline next steps or key takeaways.

Example

A counselor helping a client through financial Hardship. They Listen to the client's story, Empathize with the stress, Ask Questions to clarify the root causes, Paraphrase to ensure understanding, and Summarize the next steps to alleviate suffering.
